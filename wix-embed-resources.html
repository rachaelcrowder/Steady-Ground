About the Resource Hub

Built to support you after you leave the room.

The hardest part of this work often happens not in the training but weeks later — the moment something goes sideways in your session and you can't quite remember which CARE Protocol step to reach for, or you're preparing a presentation and want to revisit the evidence-not-exposure posture. The resource hub exists for those moments.

New resources are added throughout the cohort year. When the Community of Practice session produces materials — case studies you workshopped together, referrals you updated, tools you adapted — those are added here too. The hub grows with the cohort's practice.
